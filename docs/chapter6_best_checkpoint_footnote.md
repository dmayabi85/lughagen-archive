# Chapter 6 — Footnote for Best-Checkpoint Analysis

## Placement

To be inserted at the bottom of Chapter 6's introduction (before Section
6.1) OR as a footnote to the main leaderboard table.

## Text

> **Footnote**: Throughout Chapter 6, BPC values are reported as
> final-checkpoint (step-6000) numbers for methodological consistency
> across model families and cross-baseline comparison. Best-checkpoint
> selection at earlier training steps yields marginal improvement
> (0-0.010 BPC) for a subset of models; see Appendix 5.A for
> comprehensive best-checkpoint analysis, including definitive per-model
> and per-language BPC at optimal checkpoint step.

## Rationale

Preserves Chapter 6 as a clean leaderboard with consistent methodology.
Reader who wants the trade-off analysis knows where to find it.
Appendix 5.A serves as the definitive record of best-checkpoint
methodology and results.
